How to use Stowly day to day. Menu names are given in Vietnamese, with English in brackets.

## Getting started

1. Open the app and create an account on the sign-in page. You can sign in later with your password or with a magic link sent by email.
2. Every new account starts with seven built-in categories and a starter location tree (Phòng khách, Phòng ngủ › Tủ quần áo, Nhà bếp › Tủ lạnh, …). Rename, move or delete them to match your home.
3. Switch language in the sidebar or in **Cài đặt (Settings)**. Your choice is saved to your account.
4. On a phone, use the browser's "Add to Home Screen" to install Stowly as an app.

## Adding items

**Quick add** (the + button):
1. Take or pick a photo. Up to 5 photos per item; they are resized on your device before upload.
2. Type a name, or tap the scan button to read a barcode.
3. Pick a category chip, set the quantity and **Báo khi còn ≤ (Alert at or below)** if you want low-stock warnings.
4. Choose a location, or create one on the spot.
5. **Lưu (Save)**, or **Lưu & thêm tiếp (Save & add another)** to keep going.

The full form adds tags, price, purchase date and shop, expiry and warranty dates, condition, notes, an item group and the category's own fields (for example, author for books).

### Barcodes
- Scan a product barcode in quick add. If you already own an item with that code, Stowly offers **Open item** or **+1 to stock** instead of creating a duplicate.
- For a new code, Stowly tries to fill in the name and category from Open Food Facts / Open Products Facts. It only fills empty fields.
- If the camera isn't available, type the code into the same dialog.

## Finding things

- **Tất cả đồ (All items):** type anything. Accents and small typos don't matter: `ao khoac`, `lightnig` and `tu quan ao` (a location) all work.
- Filter by category, location, tag, condition and alert state, and sort by relevance, name, last updated, expiry or low stock. Filters are kept in the URL, so you can bookmark a search.
- Change quantities directly in the list with the − / + stepper. Every change is recorded in the item's history.
- **Vị trí (Locations):** browse the tree. A location's page shows everything inside it, including sub-locations.

## Alerts

Stowly raises an alert when an item:
- is expired or expiring soon;
- has a warranty ending soon;
- is due for replacement;
- is low or out of stock;
- is a book lent out and overdue.

Where to see them:
- The **dashboard** shows counts, the items that need attention and low-stock bars.
- **Cảnh báo (Alerts)** lists everything. Actions: **Mark replaced**, **Used up** (archives the item), **Add to shopping list**, **Snooze 7 days**. Each can be undone.
- A **daily digest** arrives by email and/or push notification at your chosen hour (07:00 by default). Each reminder is sent once. Expiry reminders go out at 30, 7 and 1 days before.

Change lead times, the digest hour and channels in **Cài đặt (Settings)**. You can also turn on push for the current device and send a test notification there.

## Shopping list

- On the dashboard, **Tạo danh sách cần mua (Create shopping list)** adds every low-stock item.
- You can also add items from Alerts or add free-text entries.
- Tick an entry when bought: the amount bought is added back to the item's stock.

## Item groups

Use a group when several items count together, e.g. all your charging cables.

1. Open **Nhóm đồ (Groups)** and create a group with a threshold (e.g. "Dây sạc", 5).
2. Add items to it, or pick the group in the item form.
3. When the combined quantity falls to the threshold, the group shows up on the dashboard, on Alerts, in the digest and can be added to the shopping list. Buying a group entry restocks the member updated most recently.

## QR labels

1. **Vị trí (Locations) → In nhãn QR (Print QR labels)**.
2. Pick locations and a label size: 63.5×38.1 mm (3 per row) or 99.1×67.7 mm (2 per row) on A4 sticker paper.
3. Print at 100% scale and stick the labels on boxes and shelves.
4. Scan a label with any phone camera, or with the scan button on the dashboard, to see what's inside. You'll be asked to sign in first if needed.

## Import and export

- **Import:** **Cài đặt → Nhập từ CSV (Import from CSV)**. Excel files saved as CSV (semicolon-separated), Vietnamese or English headers and dd/mm/yyyy dates all work.
  - Check the column matching and preview, then choose what happens to duplicates: update, skip or create again.
  - Missing categories, locations, groups and tags are created.
  - **Tải tệp mẫu (Download template)** gives you a ready-made file.
- **Export:** CSV of all items, or a full JSON backup, in **Cài đặt (Settings)**. Re-importing an exported CSV updates the same items.

## Spending

**Chi tiêu (Spending)** shows, for the chosen period:
- total spent compared with the previous period, number of purchases and average per month;
- a monthly chart, with a table view;
- spending by category and by room;
- the most expensive purchases.

Items without a price or purchase date aren't counted; a note says how many were left out.

## Clean-up suggestions

**Gợi ý dọn dẹp (Clean-up ideas)** has four tabs:
- **Unused:** no quantity change, move or condition change for a year. The threshold can be changed in Settings.
- **Worn** and **Broken:** by condition.
- **Duplicates:** same name in the same location.

Actions: **Give away**, **Archive**, **Keep**, **Snooze 30 days**, and **Merge** for duplicates, which adds the quantities to the oldest entry.
